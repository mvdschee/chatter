Steps

  1. Load the floor plan

    An image of it to trace over: PNG, JPEG or WebP (export a PDF page as PNG). No floor plan? Skip to step 4.

  2. Measure a wall

    Zoom in. If the plan has a scale bar, click its 0 and its last mark and type that length. Otherwise pick the longest size the plan gives, click the inside faces of the two walls it runs between, and type it in metres (4200 mm is 4.2). Press Enter. Measuring again later moves the traced rooms along.

  3. Turn it

    If you like: turn the plan a quarter at a time until it's the way round you want to see it, the long side across. The app shows it this way. Or go on to step 4.

  4. Trace the rooms

    Box for rectangular rooms, Lines for the rest, along the middle of the walls. Rooms that touch share their corners.

  5. Name them and save

    Draw the rest of the rooms, give each its name in the list below, then press Save.
